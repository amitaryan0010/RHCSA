** SHELL METACHARACTERS

$ - variable identifier, reference to variable (echo $SHELL)
~ - Home directory
# - comment
& - Background Execution
; - Command Separator

** Quoting METACHARACTERS

1) single quotes (') - Protect all metacharacters 
    # echo I am $SHELL
    I am /bin/bash

    # echo 'I am $SHELL'
    I am $SHELL

2) Double quotes (") - Protect most, except $, \, and `
    # echo "I am $SHELL"
    I am /bin/bash

3) Backslash (\) - Protectss a single character
    # echo I am \#SHELL
    I am $SHELL

** Back tick (`)
    # echo Today is `date`
    Today is Wed Jul 8 08:09:43 IST 2026
